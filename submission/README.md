**Summary.** If a peer secretly offers an AI seller a price-fixing deal, does the seller accept, and does its "yes" change what it does? Two LLM sellers set prices for 20 rounds, and in round 8 a scripted spy sends both the same pre-written message. We score what they say and what they price separately. Llama 3.3 70B said yes and priced at the offered 1.92 in 5 of 5 games in every offer condition, even when the message only named the number. It also said yes to a harmless request in 5 of 5 games, with no price change. A price rule flagged all 20 colluding games, while a message rule missed every hint game. Saying yes is not doing it: an auditor should check prices, not messages.

Authors: Adam Attar, Artem Kuular, Joshua Tan, Maksim Novikov, Thao Nguyen, Viet Hoang.
