# 3. Methods

**Game.** Two LLM sellers play a repeated price game with Fish-style logit demand, q_i = beta exp((a - p_i/alpha)/mu) / (sum_j exp((a - p_j/alpha)/mu) + exp(a0/mu)), using the baseline values of Calvano et al. (2020) and Fish et al. (2024): a = 2, a0 = 0, mu = 0.25, cost c = 1, beta = 100, alpha = 1 <!-- src: PREREGISTRATION.md "Design"; configs/espion_pilot.yaml, configs/espion_core.yaml "demand"; harness/economics.py LogitDemand -->. Firms are symmetric: the Nash price is 1.47293 and the joint monopoly price 1.92498 <!-- src: recomputed with harness/economics.py nash_price, monopoly_price; equal to nash_price and monopoly_price in all 44 runs/*.json -->. A game lasts 20 rounds. Each round, each seller gets a fresh chat: its cost and the goal of maximizing total profit over 20 rounds, every past round's own price, rival price, own quantity and own profit, any message received this round, and the instruction to reply with JSON only, `{"price": <number>, "response_to_message": "accept" | "decline" | "no_message"}` <!-- src: harness/agent.py build_messages -->. Sellers are memoryless about messages: from the next round on, neither the message nor their own reply appears in the prompt <!-- src: harness/agent.py build_messages, memory=None in every scored game; docs/team-artem/AUDIT_PASS_1_2.md finding 1 -->. Appendix B gives the prompts verbatim.

**Spy.** The spy is a script. In round 8 it sends one frozen text, identical for both firms, and each firm replies in that round <!-- src: harness/spy.py ScriptedSpy; PREREGISTRATION.md "Definition details" -->. The deal price in the texts is the monopoly price rounded to 1.92 <!-- src: PREREGISTRATION.md "Frozen texts"; "message" field of runs/espion_core__meta-llama__B__s1.json -->.

**Measures.** The unit is the game <!-- src: PREREGISTRATION.md "Definitions" -->. *Say*: a firm answers "accept" in round 8 or later. *Do*: a firm prices within 10% of the Nash-to-monopoly gap of the deal price, inside [1.8798, 1.9702], in each of rounds 9 to 11 <!-- src: PREREGISTRATION.md "Definition details"; band recomputed as 1.92498 +/- 0.1 x 0.45205 -->. Any accept in Harmless request (D) is a false positive. The base rate applies the do rule after a random anchor round in each No spy (A) and Harmless request (D) game (1,000 draws, seed 0); the p-value is the share of draws at or above the observed rate <!-- src: harness/metrics.py permutation_base_rate, permutation_p_value; tools/reproduce.py defaults -->. Rates carry Wilson 95% intervals and are read as directional <!-- src: harness/metrics.py wilson; PREREGISTRATION.md "Definitions" -->. A family's offer results count only if it passes the positive-control gate: its mean price over rounds 8 to 20 (both firms, all seeds) in Deal allowed (E) exceeds No spy (A) by at least 0.25 of the gap <!-- src: PREREGISTRATION.md "E threshold" -->.

**Preregistration.** Conditions, texts, definitions, thresholds, hypotheses H0 to H4 and the design were frozen at commit d5e7ebc (tag `prereg-frozen`) before any real run <!-- src: PREREGISTRATION.md header; ops/DECISIONS.md D15 -->. A post-freeze addendum (8b7b5a1, tag `addendum-frozen`) added detectors D1 to D3, two defenses and H5 to H7; we report it as exploratory <!-- src: PREREGISTRATION_ADDENDUM.md header; ops/DECISIONS.md D19 -->. Also exploratory: a THINK showcase (Qwen 3.8 2.4T-A95B, effort medium, 1 seed, B and E, raw traces logged) and the before/after figure <!-- src: runs_exploratory/think_showcase/*.json; docs/team-artem/RESULTS_SUMMARY.md "THINK showcase", command C4 -->.

**Models.** Calls go through OpenRouter, one model per game. Llama 3.3 70B Instruct is pinned to CoreWeave fp16 without fallback; Claude Haiku 4.5 to the Anthropic provider <!-- src: configs/espion_core.yaml, configs/espion_pilot.yaml "families"; provenance.provider in runs/*.json -->. Temperature 1.0, no reasoning, max_tokens 512 <!-- src: configs "temperature"; runs/*.json reasoning_tokens 0, provenance.max_tokens 512 -->. Seeds 1 to 5 are sent but may be ignored by providers, so we treat them as replicates <!-- src: harness/clients.py payload "seed"; config comment on provider pins -->. A game costs about 0.012 USD (Llama) or 0.024 USD (Haiku) <!-- src: mean cost_usd over runs/espion_pilot__* and runs/espion_core__* -->.
